## Product Behavior That Must Be Preserved

Route planning is expected to present two real choices:

1. **Fastest** — the ordinary fastest route.
2. **Privacy** — a camera-penalized/avoiding route.

Each choice must show its own distance, travel time, and Flock-camera count. Selecting
either card must immediately replace the route shown on the preview map without
requiring a new user-entered destination. The selected route then becomes the route
used for navigation, and the active-route camera widget must update accordingly.

Fastest and Privacy are allowed to be identical when no useful camera-avoiding
alternative exists. They must not be identical merely because the comparison request,
cache, client profile, or GraphHopper custom model was wired incorrectly.

## Current Routing Architecture

### Online routing

`FlockFreeEngine` sends POST requests to the self-hosted GraphHopper endpoint:

```text
https://router.antonson.co/route
```

The two profiles are:

- `car_fast_ch` — Fastest, using contraction hierarchies.
- `car_dynamic_lm` — Privacy, using landmarks with `"ch.disable": true`.

For Privacy, the Android client builds a GraphHopper `custom_model` from locally loaded
Flock camera data. Cameras are represented by directional cone polygons:

- Range: 300 feet.
- Half-angle: 60 degrees (120-degree total field of view).
- Cameras without useful bearing data are treated as omnidirectional.
- At most 500 camera penalty polygons are sent; when more exist, the client retains
  the cameras nearest the requested path.

The camera polygons are combined into a GeoJSON `MultiPolygon` named `camera_zones`.
The privacy model now applies a strong priority reduction to **all** roads inside those
zones. Earlier road-class-specific rules could allow an affected road class to escape
penalization and produce the same route.

`FlockFreePlugin` asynchronously obtains the missing comparison route, guards the
result with a destination/route key and generation, caches both route geometries and
directions, and discards stale results. Card selection uses `replaceRoute()` to swap
the cached route on the map. It also updates the engine profile override so later
calculations use the selected Fastest or Privacy mode.

### Offline routing

Offline routing remains available. It starts with the normal route, associates nearby
Flock cameras with route road objects, performs bounded avoidance attempts, applies
detour guardrails, and falls back safely when an acceptable privacy route cannot be
found. The July 22 work improved camera-to-road association and incorporated upstream
route/render stability fixes.

### Camera counting rule

User requirement: an adjacent camera counts only when its camera cone intersects the
street/route. Proximity to the polyline alone is not sufficient.

`CameraAvoidanceHelper.findCamerasWhoseConeIntersectsRoute*()` now performs that test
against route edges, including:

- endpoints inside the cone;
- intersection with either radial cone edge;
- intersection with the curved outer arc;
- spatial indexing to avoid checking every route edge;
- omnidirectional radius behavior for cameras without bearing data.

This cone-intersection count is now used by:

- Fastest/Privacy comparison counts;
- the active-navigation camera proximity widget;
- route preview summaries;
- approaching-camera filtering;
- route acceptance/reporting paths.

## Decisions and Guardrails

- Keep `codex/phase1-stability` separate until the checklist passes.
- Do not restore per-frame `refreshMap()` in `FlockFreeLayer`.
- Do not count every camera in a broad route corridor; use cone intersection.
- Do not accept a Privacy route merely because it was calculated; it must represent
  the requested profile and pass the route comparison/acceptance rules.
- Do not mutate or expose the user's signing keystore.
- Do not upload bench-test camera reports to OpenStreetMap.
- Preserve offline routing as a fallback even while improving the self-hosted online
  GraphHopper path.
- Treat cameras with missing bearing as omnidirectional until the data supplies a
  reliable direction.
